# Release Notes - v1.0.0 (College MVP)
RentKart v1.0.0 is the complete college MVP launch.

## Major Capabilities
- Multi-vendor marketplace support
- Role-based authentication (Admin, Vendor, Staff, Customer)
- Complete Equipment & Inventory management
- Real-time availability checking and conflict prevention
- End-to-end Booking and Checkout flow
- Payment and Security Deposit tracking
- Full Rental Lifecycle (Issue, Return, Damage Assessment)
- Real-time Notifications via SignalR
- Financial Reports and Analytics Dashboards
- Customer Reviews and Wishlist

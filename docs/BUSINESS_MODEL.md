# Business Model
RentKart is a peer-to-peer B2C and C2C marketplace connecting local equipment owners (vendors) with renters (customers).

## Current MVP
The platform acts as a lead generation and booking management tool. Renters browse, pay, and pick up equipment locally. No home delivery is provided.

## Future Vision (v2)
- **Commission System**: RentKart will charge a percentage fee on every successful booking.
- **Insurance**: Offer accidental damage waivers.
- **Home Delivery**: Last-mile logistics integration.
